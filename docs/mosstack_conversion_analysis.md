## 1. SECTION-BY-SECTION BREAKDOWN (Exact Order)

- **Nav links (left-center):** 精選案例 (Portfolio) | 服務項目 (Services) | 工作室 (Studio) | 聯絡 (Contact)

- **Headline:** "不只是把網站做出來 是把它做到真的能用" — "Not just building a website, but making it truly usable." Strong, benefit-driven headline.

- **Assessment:** Strong headline, but the hero is text-only. No visual proof of work above the fold.

## 9. FRICTION POINTS FOR POTENTIAL CUSTOMERS

1. **No contact form** — Business owners who don't use WhatsApp or LINE have no way to reach out.
2. **No email address** — A major barrier for B2B buyers who expect email.
3. **No phone number** — Some business owners prefer calling.
4. **No pricing** — Even a "starting from" price would reduce hesitation.
5. **No client logos** — No recognizable brand names to build credibility.
6. **No team photos** — The "about" section has no team photos, making it feel impersonal.
7. **No project count or years in business** — No quantitative social proof.
8. **No case study screenshots in the list view** — Cards show only text, requiring a click to see visuals.
9. **Testimonials lack photos** — Anonymous testimonials feel less credible.
10. **No FAQ section** — Common questions (timeline, pricing range, process) are unanswered.

---

## 10. OVERALL FIRST IMPRESSION & CONVERSION ASSESSMENT

### ✅ STRENGTHS
- **Strong headline** — Benefit-focused, not feature-focused. Immediately communicates value.
- **Clear differentiation** — "Fixed pricing," "Live Demo," "Full-stack" are compelling differentiators.
- **Low-friction CTA** — "Free 30-min consultation" is low-commitment and well-positioned.
- **Live Demo availability** — A rare and powerful trust signal for a dev studio.
- **Clean, fast-loading design** — No clutter, fast to scan.
- **Floating WhatsApp/LINE buttons** — Always accessible, reduces friction for mobile users.
- **"Currently accepting projects" badge** — Creates subtle urgency.

### ❌ WEAKNESSES
- **No contact form or email** — Forces all contact through messaging apps, which some business owners find informal.
- **No pricing** — Even a range would help qualify leads and reduce wasted inquiries.
- **No client logos** — Zero recognizable brand names to build credibility.
- **No team photos** — The "about" section is text-only, missing a human element.
- **No quantitative social proof** — No "X projects completed," no years in business.
- **No FAQ** — No answers to common objections (timeline, cost, what's included).
- **No case study thumbnails** — Portfolio cards are text-only on the homepage.
- **No testimonials with photos** — Reduces credibility.
- **No phone number** — Limits accessibility for traditional business owners.
- **No blog or content** — No SEO content to attract organic traffic or build authority.

### 🎯 TOP 3 CONVERSION RECOMMENDATIONS
1. **Add a contact form** alongside WhatsApp/LINE in the contact section — capture leads who prefer email.
2. **Add pricing guidance** — Even "Projects typically start from NT$X" would dramatically reduce hesitation.
3. **Add client logos or project count** — "Trusted by X businesses" or a logo strip would significantly boost credibility.
